Modified Leading Edge Aerodynamic Analysis

Wind tunnel validation and flow visualization of leading edge geometry effects

Overview

Evaluated modified leading edge geometries to quantify impacts on lift, drag, and surface flow behavior. Conducted controlled wind tunnel testing and oil flow visualization, then compared trends against analytical expectations to support repeatable experimental procedures and future design iterations.

My Role

  • Prepared and iterated leading edge configurations for testing, ensuring consistent geometry and installation
  • Executed wind tunnel runs and oil flow visualization to capture aerodynamic trends and surface flow behavior
  • Processed and compared test data across configurations to identify performance changes and repeatability

Tools Used

Wind Tunnel Testing Oil Flow Visualization MATLAB XFOIL

Key Results

  • Identified leading edge geometry driven shifts in lift and drag trends across test conditions
  • Documented repeatable surface flow features using oil flow visualization to support interpretation of performance changes
  • Established a consistent test and data reduction workflow to enable rapid iteration on future configurations
