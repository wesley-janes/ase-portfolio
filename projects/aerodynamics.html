Overview

Combined low-speed wind tunnel testing with computational and analytical comparison to evaluate aerodynamic performance trends. Focused on modified leading-edge geometries, repeatable testing procedures, and a clean data pipeline from acquisition to results.

What I Did

  • Designed and prepared multiple leading-edge configurations for subsonic tunnel testing
  • Executed repeatable wind tunnel runs and verified setup consistency and data quality
  • Reduced and visualized data in MATLAB to compare performance across configurations
  • Correlated experimental trends against computational tools such as XFOIL
