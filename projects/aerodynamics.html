Applied Aerodynamics Research

Aerodynamic Analyses of Modified Leading Edges

Experimental validation of leading edge geometry effects using wind tunnel testing and flow visualization

Scroll

Overview

Investigated the aerodynamic performance of modified leading edge geometries through controlled subsonic wind tunnel testing. Quantified changes in lift, drag, and surface flow behavior using experimental measurements and oil flow visualization. Results were correlated with XFOIL predictions to validate repeatability, data fidelity, and aerodynamic trends across configurations.

Photos

My Role

  • Designed and prepared modified leading edge geometries for experimental testing, ensuring consistent geometry and installation
  • Executed subsonic wind tunnel tests and oil flow visualization to capture aerodynamic performance and surface flow behavior
  • Developed MATLAB post processing scripts to automate lift, drag, and moment coefficient calculations

Tools Used

Wind Tunnel Testing Oil Flow Visualization MATLAB XFOIL Data Reduction Experimental Validation

Key Results

  • Validated aerodynamic repeatability of modified leading edge geometries within ±5 percent of XFOIL predictions
  • Identified geometry driven changes in lift and drag behavior across multiple test configurations
  • Reduced data processing time by automating aerodynamic coefficient extraction and post processing workflows
