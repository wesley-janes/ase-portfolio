Rocket Nozzle Compressible Flow Analysis

Converging diverging nozzle modeling, choking conditions, and performance estimation

Scroll

Overview

Analyzed compressible flow through a converging diverging rocket nozzle to predict choking behavior, exit conditions, and performance metrics. Combined isentropic flow relations with numerical calculations to evaluate the effects of area ratio and chamber conditions on Mach number, pressure, and thrust related quantities.

Photos

My Role

  • Built a nozzle analysis workflow using isentropic relations to solve for throat and exit conditions
  • Computed Mach number, pressure, temperature, and density across the nozzle for varying area ratios
  • Evaluated performance sensitivity to chamber conditions and documented assumptions and limitations

Tools Used

MATLAB Compressible Flow Isentropic Relations Data Visualization

Key Results

  • Predicted choking conditions at the throat and identified exit Mach number trends versus area ratio
  • Quantified nozzle state changes from chamber to exit to support performance estimation and comparison
  • Delivered a reusable calculation template for rapid evaluation of nozzle designs and operating points
