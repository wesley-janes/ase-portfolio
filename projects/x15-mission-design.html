X-15 Mission Design and Analysis

Hypersonic vehicle mission planning, trajectory analysis, and system integration

Scroll

Overview

Developed a mission level design and analysis for the NASA X-15 hypersonic research aircraft, focusing on trajectory definition, vehicle performance, and system level constraints. The project integrated historical flight data with analytical modeling to evaluate feasibility, operational limits, and mission objectives.

Photos

My Role

  • Defined mission objectives, constraints, and performance requirements for the X-15 flight profile
  • Performed trajectory and vehicle performance analysis across hypersonic flight regimes
  • Integrated aerodynamic, thermal, and operational considerations into a cohesive mission design

Tools Used

MATLAB ANSYS STK NASA Technical Reports

Key Results

  • Established a feasible hypersonic mission profile consistent with historical X-15 flight data
  • Identified thermal and aerodynamic constraints governing peak Mach number and altitude
  • Demonstrated system level tradeoffs between performance, heating, and mission objectives
